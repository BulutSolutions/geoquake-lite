Install GeoQuake Lite

Live earthquakes on a desk AMOLED.

Warning: installing erases everything on the board: any other firmware and its settings, saved Wi-Fi networks, and files in its flash.

You need a Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-2.16, a USB-C cable, and Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer. Connect the board before you press the button.

After flashing, use Connect to Wi-Fi in the installer to join your network.

To change Wi-Fi later, choose Settings > Forget Wi-Fi on the device, then open this installer page again and choose Connect to Wi-Fi.

Read the README for what is in the box, how to build from source, and the map pack.
